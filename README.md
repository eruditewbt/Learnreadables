# Learnreadables

> Read, analyze, and learn from the materials that matter.

## The Opportunity

Learnreadables brings EruditeWBT’s PDF Reader and ReadAndAnalyze experiences together. It helps people work through documents as active learning material: read, find what matters, ask questions, analyze ideas, and return to useful information.

## Who It Serves

Students, researchers, educators, and professionals who learn from documents and other substantial reading.

## What It Makes Possible

- Read and work with PDF learning materials
- Explore documents through analysis and questions
- Support study, review, and deeper understanding from reading

## How People Use It

1. Choose a document relevant to what you are learning.
2. Read and explore its important ideas.
3. Use analysis and review to strengthen your understanding.

## How It Connects

- Complements LearnedData: reading and analysis build context, while assessment supports practice and review.
