# Learnreadables

> Read deeply. Find what matters. Keep moving.

## The Opportunity

Learnreadables brings reading and study materials into a more useful learning experience, helping people spend less time wrestling with documents and more time understanding them.

## Who It Serves

Students, researchers, educators, and professionals working with substantial documents.

## What It Makes Possible

- Bring document reading and study into one experience
- Explore material through questions and guided analysis
- Support review from a range of educational resources

## How People Use It

1. Bring a learning document into the workspace.
2. Read it and explore ideas relevant to your goal.
3. Use questions and analysis to deepen understanding.

## How It Connects

- Complements EruditeWBT courses by helping learners engage closely with supporting materials.
